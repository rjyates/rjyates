# Technology icon sources

The technology silhouettes are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), retrieved October 22, 2026, and distributed under its [CC0 dedication](LICENSE-SIMPLE-ICONS.md). They are placed in custom Catppuccin-inspired tiles. Brand marks remain associated with their respective owners; their inclusion does not imply endorsement.

Link-button illustrations in `../buttons/` are custom SVG line drawings. These assets require no scripts, analytics, or remote badge-rendering service.
